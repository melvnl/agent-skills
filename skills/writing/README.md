# Writing

Content workflows. The engineering blog pipeline runs in this order, each step gated on your approval:

`blog-interviewer` → `blog-architect` → *(draft)* → `blog-visual-storyteller` → `blog-editor`

- **[blog-interviewer](blog-interviewer/SKILL.md)**: Extract the story and evidence through a one-question-at-a-time interview.
- **[blog-architect](blog-architect/SKILL.md)**: Thesis, narrative structure, and outline from the interview summary.
- **[blog-visual-storyteller](blog-visual-storyteller/SKILL.md)**: SVG diagrams and memes, embedded with `ImageCaption`.
- **[blog-editor](blog-editor/SKILL.md)**: Editorial, technical, and publication review of a draft.
